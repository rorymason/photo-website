The question

"How might we create a more meaningful experience around listening to recorded music?"

Why formats

My literature review showed that every format comes with its own affordances, constraints and rituals, and these shape how much attention people pay to music. Looking through the lens of formats let me compare real listening experiences and turn them into design opportunities.

Method

Listening to how people listen

I set research objectives around why people choose a format, what motivates them and what makes an experience feel more meaningful. Semi-structured interviews captured people's own stories, and watching them use their formats showed how they actually behave.

I recruited seven people who actively switch between streaming and at least one physical format, and wrote my interview guide from the research objectives and the literature.

Informed consent

Everyone knew what the study was for and agreed to take part.

Freedom to stop

Participants could pause or pull out at any point.

Control afterwards

Answers were anonymised, and participants could edit or remove anything they'd said.
