The question

“How might we create a more meaningful experience around listening to recorded music?”

Why formats

My literature review showed that each format creates its own affordances, constraints and rituals — and these shape how much attention people pay to music. Using formats as a lens let me compare real listening experiences and turn them into design opportunities.

Method

Listening to how people listen

I set objectives around why people choose a format, what motivates them, and what makes an experience feel more meaningful. Semi‑structured interviews captured participants’ own stories; observing them with their formats showed how they actually behave.

I recruited seven people who actively choose between streaming and at least one physical format, and wrote interview guidelines from the research objectives and literature.

Informed consent

Everyone knew what the study was for and agreed to take part.

Freedom to stop

Participants could pause or withdraw at any point.

Control afterwards

Responses were anonymised, and participants could edit or remove what they’d said.
