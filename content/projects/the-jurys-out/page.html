The brief

Down to earth and plain speaking, in a category that usually leans sweet and fluffy.

What I'm making

The brand covers a wordmark, typefaces, colour and a short usage guide. The website is responsive and usable by keyboard and screen reader, and introduces the programme, a free check-in and a dedicated safety and support page.

Design rules

Designing for a vulnerable moment

People come to the programme at a hard time in their lives, so the design follows a few firm rules.

Safety is one tap away

Safety information is always one tap away, even with the companion open.

A guide, not a person

The AI programme companion has to read clearly as a guide, not as a person or a support service.

No scores

Nothing on the site counts, scores or gamifies progress.
