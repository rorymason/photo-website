Designing for the hand

A deck that fits under your thumbs

Everything you touch on a real turntable had to work on a small glass screen — so each control is large, colour‑coded and placed where your hands already are.

Scratch the platter

Drag on the platter to scratch. Playback follows how fast your finger turns — one full turn a second is normal speed — and moving nearer the centre scratches faster, like a real record.

Cue and play

Big CUE and play buttons sit at the bottom of the deck, in the app’s pink and teal, so they read at a glance mid‑mix.

Pitch and nudge

A pitch fader with a selectable range, plus − and + nudge buttons to pull a track back into time.

Know the tempo

The waveform shows your cue point. BPM is detected automatically — or type it in, or tap eight beats. Swipe the BPM display to see the pitch percentage instead.
